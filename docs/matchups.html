No history between two teams shows as no meetings on record — never a guessed number.

How the projection works. Each number blends the player's career average with this season's observed average, shifted only 20% of the way from one to the other (never a full override from a single game's result) — the same dampening principle proven on the MLB build, tuned for NFL's slower weekly cadence. Projections are frozen the moment this page was built and won't silently change mid-week. Not yet validated against real graded results, and not yet compared against a market line since none is wired in.
