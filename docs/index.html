  • Real advanced stats throughout: EPA/CPOE for passing, RYOE for rushing, separation/YAC for receiving — all genuine tracking-data metrics, not derived guesses.
  • Full grading system live: every prediction frozen once, graded against the real result, with a real calibration loop feeding corrections back into future projections once enough graded history exists.
  • Matchups page: the next date's real games, with real historical performance vs. that exact opponent, depth-chart-validated starters, and the real Kalshi market for that specific game.

What's intentionally not faked

  • Most calibration corrections still show "insufficient data" — MIN_SAMPLE=30 real graded entries per stat/tier are required before a correction applies, and the season is still young.
  • Kicking was dropped. Field goal attempts depend too heavily on decisions (coach's 4th-down call, weather, game script) that this build has no honest way to project yet — better to drop it than publish a number with no real basis.
  • Touchdown grading uses a synthetic 0.5-TD breakeven threshold rather than a real market line, since Kalshi's touchdown markets are probability-based, not point-value lines.
