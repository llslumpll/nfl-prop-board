How this page works & what's real (click to expand)
Provisional tiers only. One week of sample isn't enough for a calibrated model yet — see the Home page for what's real and what's not.
Next-game projections are now opponent-adjusted. The number reflects the opponent's real yards allowed this season vs. the league average, dampened the same way every other correction on this site is. Hover the projected number for the pre-adjustment value and the real numbers behind the factor.
RYOE (Rushing Yards Over Expected) is a real NFL Next Gen Stat, not a derived guess — a tracking-data model predicts expected yards for every carry based on the blocking/space actually created, and RYOE is the real gap between that and what the back actually gained. Positive means he's outrunning his blocking; negative means he's underperforming it, regardless of the raw yardage total. Stacked-box % shows how often he faced 8+ defenders in the box — useful context for reading RYOE (succeeding despite a stacked box is a stronger signal than the same RYOE against a light box).
