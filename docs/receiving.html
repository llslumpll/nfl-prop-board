How this page works & what's real (click to expand)
Provisional tiers only. Same caveat as Passing — one week of sample isn't enough for a calibrated model yet. Where a receiver's own quarterback appears in the table, that pairing is flagged as correlated: their props move together, so stacking both as if they were independent legs is a common mistake worth catching before it happens.
Real wind data now factors into the passing projection here too — receiving depends on the same pass attempts, so the same live wind adjustment applied on Passing carries through. Never applied at domes/fixed-roof stadiums.
Next-game projections are now opponent-adjusted. The number reflects the opponent's real yards allowed this season vs. the league average, dampened the same way every other correction on this site is. Hover the projected number for the pre-adjustment value and the real numbers behind the factor.
Separation and YAC Above Expectation are real NFL Next Gen Stats, tracking-data-derived. Separation is real distance from the nearest defender at the catch point (NFL average is roughly 2.7–3.0 yards). YAC+ (Yards After Catch Above Expectation) isolates a receiver's own ability to create extra yards after the catch, separate from what the play design or blocking gave him — a receiver can win with tight separation if he creates real YAC+ on his own, which is worth reading alongside separation rather than in place of it.
