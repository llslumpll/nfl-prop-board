xTD Debt Watch

Real Expected Touchdowns (xTD): every one of a player's real touches this season, priced by the real historical (2023-2025) TD conversion rate for that exact field position and play type, summed and compared against real actual TDs scored. Pulled from the full real rushing/receiving volume pools, not just recent scorers — a real high-opportunity player stuck at zero TDs is exactly who this exists to find, and wouldn't otherwise show up anywhere on this page. Positive debt = the ball has found him in scoring position more than the scoreboard shows (regression-up). Negative = he's converting above what real opportunity alone predicts (regression-down risk). Requires 15+ real priced touches and a 1.0+ real gap before showing anything.
