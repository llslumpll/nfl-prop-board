    How this page works & what's real (click to expand)
    Provisional tiers only. With one week of data, there isn't enough sample to run a real calibrated model. The tier column reflects sample size, not a tuned prediction — see the project brief's open question on NFL's weekly MIN_SAMPLE/DAMPEN values.
    Real wind data now factors into the passing projection — live forecast for the actual stadium and date (free, no-key Open-Meteo API, same mechanism already proven on the MLB build). Never applied at domes/fixed-roof stadiums. Below 10 mph wind has no effect; the reduction above that is a reasoned first-cut estimate, not yet empirically validated against this site's own graded history.
    Next-game projections are now opponent-adjusted. The number reflects the opponent's real yards allowed this season vs. the league average, dampened the same way every other correction on this site is (never a full override, especially with defenses still on a 1-2 game sample). Hover the projected number for the pre-adjustment value and the real numbers behind the factor.
    EPA/play and CPOE are real advanced metrics, not derived guesses — nflreadpy provides both directly. EPA/play (Expected Points Added per dropback) measures real scoring impact, not just yards: a 3-yard gain on 3rd-and-2 helps a lot; the same 3 yards on 3rd-and-10 doesn't. CPOE (Completion % Over Expected) isolates a QB's own accuracy from how open his receivers were. League-average EPA/play runs roughly 0.00–0.10; CPOE roughly ±3 is a meaningful edge either direction.
    Time to Throw, Aggressiveness, and Air Yards Differential are real NFL Next Gen Stats — tracking-data-derived, a separate real source from the box-score stats above. Time to Throw is seconds from snap to release. Aggressiveness is the share of throws into tight coverage (a defender within 1 yard at the catch point) — not inherently good or bad, just a real style signal. Air Yards Differential is intended depth minus completed depth: a strongly negative number means a QB is throwing deep more than he's actually completing deep.
