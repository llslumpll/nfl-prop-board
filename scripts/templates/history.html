Best 5 Track Record

Two different questions, tracked separately, same as the accuracy-by-tier split above. Highest Confidence is the model's own most-likely-to-hit ranking — real probability, using each player's own game-to-game variance (or real Poisson probability for touchdowns). Best Value is the biggest gap between our projection and the real market line — where the market looks most wrong. These can and often will name different players in the same week. The goal stated in the project brief: watch these numbers climb toward a consistent 4/5 or 5/5 week over week, not just show a good week once.

Team Markets

Real game-outcome predictions — moneyline, spread and total — frozen from our own real net-points-per-game model against FanDuel's real line at that same moment, then graded against the real final score once the game finishes. A real tie (moneyline) or a pick frozen with no real FanDuel line to compare against counts as graded once the game is final, but never counts toward the hit rate — there's nothing real to call it right or wrong against.
