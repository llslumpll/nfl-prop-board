What's real right now

  • Full stat-page coverage: Passing, Receiving, Receptions, Rushing, Kicking, and a cross-position Touchdowns board — all from live nflreadpy data.
  • Matchups page: real upcoming games from the live schedule, with each team's top passer/rusher/receiver's actual historical performance against that specific opponent (2023–2025) — or an honest "no meetings on record" where two teams haven't played recently.
  • Injury report status pulled in per player, where reported.
  • QB–receiver correlation flagged on Receiving and Receptions, since stacking a QB with his own WR1 is a common, easy mistake.

What's intentionally not faked

  • No calibrated confidence score. A first-cut MIN_SAMPLE=30 / DAMPEN=0.20 has been reasoned out for NFL's weekly cadence (see scripts/dataio.py), but it hasn't been validated against real graded picks yet, so tiers shown here are still sample-size labels, not tuned predictions.
  • No Kalshi or PrizePicks market lines — not wired into this build (the sandbox this was built in can't reach those APIs; GitHub Actions runners can, so this is a natural next step).
  • No weather factor for passing yet — needs its own wind-specific derivation per the brief, not a ported formula.
  • History page is empty on purpose — no predictions exist yet to grade.
