What's real right now

  • Full stat-page coverage: Passing, Receiving, Receptions, Rushing, and a cross-position Touchdowns board — all from live nflreadpy data.
  • Matchups page: the next date's real games, with each team's top passer/rusher/receiver's actual historical performance against that specific opponent (2023–2025), plus the real Kalshi market for that exact game.
  • Best 5 above: real edge between our own dampened projection and a real market line (PrizePicks for yardage/receptions, Kalshi for touchdowns) — never shown without both sides being real.
  • Injury report status pulled in per player, where reported.
  • QB–receiver correlation flagged on Receiving and Receptions, since stacking a QB with his own WR1 is a common, easy mistake.

What's intentionally not faked

  • No calibrated confidence score. A first-cut MIN_SAMPLE=30 / DAMPEN=0.20 has been reasoned out for NFL's weekly cadence (see scripts/dataio.py), but it hasn't been validated against real graded picks yet.
  • No grading yet. Best 5's edge is real, but nothing calls a pick a hit or miss until the underlying game finishes and a grading rule is decided.
  • No weather factor for passing yet — needs its own wind-specific derivation per the brief, not a ported formula.
  • History page is empty on purpose — no predictions exist yet to grade.
  • Kicking was dropped. Field goal attempts depend too heavily on decisions (coach's 4th-down call, weather, game script) that this build has no honest way to project yet — better to drop it than publish a number with no real basis.
