Defensive coverage identity

Two different, clearly separate signals. 2025 Man/Zone is real, human-charted coverage data from last season (nflverse/FTN charting) — genuinely measured, not inferred, but not this season's numbers, since nflverse hasn't released 2026 coverage charting yet. 2026 Cushion is a live, real proxy updating every week this season — the actual average distance opposing receivers have faced at the snap against this defense. Tighter cushion generally suggests more man-coverage-like tendencies; looser suggests more zone. It's a real number, but a proxy, not charted coverage type. Logged weekly so it can be checked against real 2026 charting once nflverse publishes it.
