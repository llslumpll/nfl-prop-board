How it works

  • Logged before kickoff: picks come from each account's public posts and are added to the site before the game starts. Paid/VIP picks and anything posted after kickoff aren't counted.
  • Graded from box scores: nflverse weekly stats and snap counts. Played with no catches = 0; didn't play = void. Anytime TD counts rushing and receiving TDs (not passing).
  • Units: 1U per pick at the posted odds. Ladders: the base rung at 1U and each higher rung at the stake he gave (1U when he didn't say). Slips: 1U per slip. Jake Venom's TD Watch is priced at his app's TD % (marked *).
  • Live: once a game kicks off, yardage and reception picks show where the player is now and whether he's on pace.
